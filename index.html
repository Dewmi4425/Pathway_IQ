Literature Survey

The foundation of PathwayIQ is built on extensive research across econometric time-series analysis, natural language processing, semantic representation, and affective computing. Existing literature demonstrates that technical skills follow volatile adoption and decay lifecycles, where econometric modeling—specifically Holt-Winters Exponential Smoothing and ARIMA—effectively captures emerging industry shifts.

Furthermore, studies on regional labor economics indicate that global technology trends cascade into local software outsourcing markets (such as Sri Lanka) with a measurable lead-lag delay of 4 to 12 weeks. In career progression and document analysis, modern research emphasizes the limitations of rigid taxonomies, demonstrating that dense transformer embeddings (Sentence-BERT) and machine learning regressors provide far greater precision.

Research Gap

Current career guidance platforms suffer from fragmentation, offering isolated point solutions such as standalone resume checkers, static mock tests, or macro-level market reports that operate in complete silos.

The Challenge

  • Unlocalized Market Data: Western reports miss the 4–12 week regional adoption lag in local IT markets like Sri Lanka.
  • Static Career Guidance: Traditional career roadmaps ignore whether a technology is actively rising or dying out.
  • Opaque ATS Black Boxes: Automated screeners reject resumes without explaining missing skills or peer ranking.
  • Disconnected Interview Practice: Mock tools review only text transcripts, ignoring non-verbal stress and confidence.

Our Solution

  • Dual-Tier Trend Forecasting: Combines local (TopJobs LK) and global data with Granger lead-lag early-warning alerts.
  • Market-Gated Career Graphs: Dynamically prioritizes high-growth, rising skills to build accelerated promotion ladders.
  • Transparent Candidate Benchmarking: Delivers ATS quality scoring, skill gap analysis, and real applicant percentile rankings.
  • Affective Interview Coaching: Evaluates answer accuracy with a 5-pillar rubric while tracking webcam facial emotion in real time.

Research Problem & Solution

"Undergraduates face significant fragmentation and information asymmetry when navigating career readiness. Existing solutions fail to provide unified, localized intelligence connecting volatile industry skill trends with personalized trajectory prediction, transparent ATS benchmarking, and stress-aware mock interview coaching."

The Problem

  • Global data dominance with no regional lead-lag detection.
  • Static career trees that ignore skill velocity.
  • Opaque ATS black-box scores without peer benchmarking.
  • Pure text chatbots without non-verbal feedback.

Our Solution

  • PathwayIQ creates a unified, closed-loop AI ecosystem.
  • Synthesizes four specialized AI microservices into a single platform.
  • Forecasts market demands, generates dynamic career graphs, and delivers transparent ATS scoring.
  • Simulates adaptive technical interviews with real-time webcam emotion tracking.

Research Objectives

Our Goal

To engineer and evaluate an end-to-end intelligent platform that bridges the divide between industry labor market demands and undergraduate career readiness.

We aim to create a solution that is not only technologically advanced but also deeply integrated into the user's career journey, providing actionable insights at every step.

Module A: Skill Forecaster

Analyzes local and global job market trends to predict which IT skills will be in high demand over the next 12 weeks. Powered by time-series forecasting, it detects early market signals and warns students about emerging technologies.

Module B: Career Predictor

Maps out clear, personalized career progression paths by evaluating your current skills against more than 150 industry roles. It generates an interactive visual career graph that shows reachable promotion steps and milestone skills.

Module C: CV Job Analyzer

Extracts text from uploaded CVs and scanned documents to provide an instant ATS quality score and in-depth skill gap analysis. It benchmarks candidate readiness against 24 market role blueprints using machine learning.

Module D: Interview Intelligence

An interactive mock interview simulator that generates tailored technical and situational questions based on your resume. Using real-time webcam facial emotion analysis, it assesses non-verbal confidence and composure.

Research Methodology

The platform connects a Next.js client, an Express/TypeScript API gateway, four independently hosted Python inference services, and a Supabase (PostgreSQL) data store.

Gateway Orchestration

The client never calls a service directly; every request passes through the gateway, which authenticates the user's JSON Web Token, authorises the operation, invokes the relevant service or services, and returns a consolidated response.

Stateless AI Modules

Modules A, B and D run on FastAPI (ports 8001, 8002, 8004), and Module C runs on Flask (port 8003). The services do not call one another; all cross-module coordination is performed by the gateway, which also owns the request-chaining logic for CV upload and job-post comparison.

Data Persistence

Supabase provides authentication, a PostgreSQL database with row-level security so each user can only read their own rows, and object storage for uploaded files and consent-based recordings. Each Python service is stateless apart from its model files, so it can be restarted without affecting persisted data.
